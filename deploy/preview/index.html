About this deployment

This free static site presents the product and recorded screenshots. It has no sign-in, agents, voice gateway, database or write endpoints. The functional workspace remains on the laptop. A full cloud deployment must preserve Ship Court SQLite and the official Switch database and encryption keys; a sleeping service with disposable local storage cannot safely replace that runtime.
